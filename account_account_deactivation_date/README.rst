This module adds a **Deactivation Date** to accounts, so an account can
be phased out of the chart of accounts without being deleted.

The date drives the standard *Deprecated* flag:

- Until the date, the account stays active everywhere.
- From the date on, the account is deprecated and disappears from the
  configuration fields (journals, products, taxes, partners, fiscal
  positions, etc.). A daily scheduled action deprecates the accounts
  whose date has been reached.

Journal entries are validated against their **accounting date**:

- Entries dated on or after the deactivation date cannot use the
  account, whether they come from the user interface, an import or an
  integration.
- Entries dated before the deactivation date remain allowed, even after
  the date has passed. Period-closing adjustments and back-dated entries
  keep working, and the history is never changed.

Every inclusion, change or removal of the date is tracked in the account
chatter.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Accounting > Configuration > Chart of Accounts* and open an
   account.
2. Set the **Deactivation Date**. It must be later than the date of the
   last posted journal item of the account, and the account must not be
   used in a tax distribution.
3. When the date is today or in the past, the account is deprecated
   right away. When it is in the future, the account is deprecated by
   the daily scheduled action once the date is reached.

To reactivate an account, clear its deactivation date.

Use the *Inactive Account* and *Scheduled Deactivation* filters of the
chart of accounts to list deprecated accounts and accounts with a
deactivation planned in the future.

Ticking *Deprecated* without a deactivation date keeps the standard
behavior: the account is blocked on every accounting date, and no date
is stored. With a date set, *Deprecated* is read-only. Clear the date to
reactivate the account.

Known limitation: once the account is deprecated, changing the account
of an existing journal item to it fails, even on a back-dated draft
entry. Add a new journal item with the account instead.
