With the Brazilian localization (``l10n_br_sale``), a sale order without
a fiscal operation (for example, a rental or an internal order that does
not issue a fiscal document) shows a zero total, because the order total
is rebuilt from the fiscal lines.

This module keeps the regular total (the sum of the line totals) on sale
orders that have no fiscal operation. Orders with a fiscal operation are
not changed.
