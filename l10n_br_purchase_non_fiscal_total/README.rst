With the Brazilian localization (``l10n_br_purchase``), a purchase order
without a fiscal operation shows a zero total, because the order total
is rebuilt from the fiscal lines.

This module keeps the regular total (the sum of the line totals) on
purchase orders that have no fiscal operation. Orders with a fiscal
operation are not changed.
